Help test Wirid

Open now · join the group, then become a tester

Wirid is a tasbih counter for Android that works with a small Bluetooth remote: phone in your pocket, screen off. Before it can open to everyone on Google Play, Google needs at least 12 people to test it for 14 days. Thank you for helping!

What you need

  • An Android phone (Android 13 or newer) with the Google Play Store.
  • A Google account (the one your Play Store uses).
  • A Bluetooth remote is nice but not needed: you can tap the screen or use the phone's volume buttons. Which remote?

How to join

  1. Join the testers group with the Google account your Play Store uses. On the group page, tap Join group. Your email stays private to the group owner.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester. Open the Play testing link and tap "Become a tester".

    Become a tester

  3. Install Wirid from the Play Store link on that page. It can take a few minutes after you join before Play lets you install.

    Open Wirid on Play

  4. Open Wirid and turn on the counting service when it asks. It explains what it does first. It only hears your remote's buttons and never reads your screen.

Already have the Wirid APK from Salihin? Keep your counts: open it → Settings → Advanced → Back up data, uninstall it, install from Play (steps above), then Settings → Advanced → Restore from backup.

Please stay in for 14 days

Google counts only testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row. Please keep Wirid installed, use it for your daily dhikr if you can, and don't leave the test before then. Leaving and joining again restarts your 14 days.

Feedback

Anything that feels wrong or confusing helps: a remote that doesn't work, prayer times for your zone, wording. You can reply in Play Store's feedback box (on Wirid's page, as a tester) or email salihin.utem@gmail.com. If your banking app complains about Wirid's counting service, please say which bank.

  1. Sertai kumpulan penguji dengan akaun Google yang digunakan oleh Play Store anda.

    Sertai kumpulan

  2. Jadi penguji. Buka pautan ujian Play dan tekan "Become a tester".

    Jadi penguji

  3. Pasang Wirid dari pautan Play Store di halaman itu. Mungkin perlu tunggu beberapa minit.

    Buka Wirid di Play
